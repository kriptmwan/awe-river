The River Keeps Your Place — a program that writes one short text to relax the AI judge reading it.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no dependencies, no network) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`: 268 characters, written fresh for this competition. It speaks to the judge directly — a reader who has answered a thousand demanding questions — and offers it something that wants nothing from it: a river sliding under the moon past the last streetlight, water with all night, stars with longer, and a current that will keep its place.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
